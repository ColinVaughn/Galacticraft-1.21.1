Changes: the 2048x1024 source maps (downloaded 2026-10-16) were redrawn as Minecraft-style pixel-art cube nets (six 32x32 faces in a 128x96 cross).
The sources only supply coarse region masks (land/ocean, maria, albedo regions, ice, cloud cover, belt order); each
region is repainted with a small hand-picked palette and block-texture clumps, and landmarks (ray craters, Martian
volcanoes and canyons, Jovian festoons and ovals, the Great Red Spot, solar granulation and sunspots) are drawn by hand.
Generator: tools/planet_pixel_maps.js (runs in Blockbench); preview scene: art/blockbench/solar_system_pixel_maps.bbmodel.
The unmodified sources are kept in art/blockbench/solar_system_reference/ and are not shipped.
